GRAMMAR

Estructura del Past Continuous

03 / 12

El past continuous se forma con el auxiliar was / were + el verbo principal en -ing.

$$\text{Subject} + \underbrace{\text{was/were}}_{\text{auxiliar}} + \underbrace{\text{verb-ing}}_{\text{verbo principal}}$$ Ej: I was playing video games.
👤 Was

Se usa con I, He, She, It.

I was watching TV.
She was reading a book.
👥 Were

Se usa con You, We, They.

They were playing football.
We were listening to music.
SubjectAuxiliarExample
IwasI was swimming
He / She / ItwasShe was dancing
YouwereYou were running
WewereWe were cooking
TheywereThey were hanging out
⚠️
¡Cuidado! No olvides el -ing en el verbo principal. I was play ❌ → I was playing ✅
GRAMMAR

Formas: afirmativa, negativa e interrogativa

04 / 12
✅ Afirmativa
$$\text{S} + \text{was/were} + \text{V-ing}$$
I was watching TV.
They were playing football.
❌ Negativa
$$\text{S} + \text{wasn't/weren't} + \text{V-ing}$$
I wasn't watching TV.
They weren't playing football.
❓ Yes/No Questions
$$\text{Was/Were} + \text{S} + \text{V-ing}?$$
Was she reading?
Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.
❓ WH- Questions
$$\text{WH-} + \text{was/were} + \text{S} + \text{V-ing}?$$
What were you doing at 8 pm?
Where was he going?
✅ I was playing video games.  |  ❌ She wasn't listening to music.  |  ❓ Were they swimming?
📌
En preguntas, el auxiliar was/were va antes del sujeto. En negativas, se agrega not (wasn't / weren't).
SPELLING

Reglas de ortografía del -ING

05 / 12

Antes de escribir el verbo en -ing, revisa estas reglas. Son clave para no perder puntos en la prueba.

ReglaEjemploExcepción / Nota
Verbo normal + -ingplay → playingwatch → watching
Termina en -e → se quita la ewrite → writingmake → making
Termina en consonante-vocal-consonante (1 sílaba) → dobla consonanterun → runningswim → swimming
Termina en -ie → se cambia por ylie → lyingdie → dying
Termina en -ee → solo agrega -ingsee → seeingagree → agreeing
✅ Correcto
run → running
swim → swimming
write → writing
read → reading
❌ Incorrecto
runing ❌
swiming ❌
writeing ❌
readding ❌
💡
Truco: Si el verbo es corto y termina en CVC (consonante-vocal-consonante), dobla la última consonante: run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming.
USES

¿Cuándo se usa el Past Continuous?

06 / 12

Se usa para acciones que estaban en progreso en un momento específico del pasado. No indica que la acción terminó, sino que estaba ocurriendo.

🕐 Acción en progreso

En un momento concreto del pasado.

At 7 pm yesterday, I was having dinner.
A las 7 de ayer, estaba cenando.
⏸️ Acción interrumpida

Una acción larga es interrumpida por una corta (past simple).

I was watching TV when the phone rang.
Estaba viendo TV cuando sonó el teléfono.
🔄 Acciones simultáneas

Dos acciones ocurrían al mismo tiempo.

While I was studying, my brother was playing video games.
Mientras yo estudiaba, mi hermano jugaba videojuegos.
📖 Descripción de escena

Para describir el ambiente o contexto en una narración.

The sun was shining and the birds were singing.
El sol brillaba y los pájaros cantaban.
📌
Expresiones típicas: at 8 o'clock last night, yesterday at noon, while, when, at that moment.
CONTRAST

Past Continuous vs Past Simple

07 / 12

Este contraste es clave para la prueba. El past continuous describe la acción larga / en progreso; el past simple, la acción corta que interrumpe.

Past ContinuousPast Simple
Acción larga / en progresoAcción corta que interrumpe
was/were + verb-ingverbo en pasado (played, went, saw)
I was sleepingThe phone rang
They were playingShe arrived
🔗 Con WHEN
I was watching TV when the phone rang.
(acción larga + acción corta)
🔗 Con WHILE
While I was studying, my brother was playing video games.
(dos acciones simultáneas)
✅ I was walking when I saw my friend.
✅ When she arrived, we were cooking.
✅ While they were swimming, it started to rain.
💡
Truco: While + past continuous (acción larga)  |  When + past simple (acción corta que interrumpe).

PRACTICE

Ejercicios tipo prueba

10 / 12
📝 Ejercicio 1 — Completa con was/were + verbo-ing
1. At 9 pm, I ______ (watch) a series.
2. They ______ (not / play) football, they ______ (swim).
3. ______ she ______ (listen) to music?

Respuestas: 1) was watching · 2) weren't playing / were swimming · 3) Was / listening
📝 Ejercicio 2 — Elige past continuous o past simple
1. While I ______ (read), my sister ______ (draw).
2. I ______ (walk) when I ______ (see) my friend.
3. When he ______ (arrive), we ______ (have) dinner.

Respuestas: 1) was reading / was drawing · 2) was walking / saw · 3) arrived / were having
📝 Ejercicio 3 — Escribe la pregunta
1. What / you / do / at 8 pm yesterday?
2. Where / they / go / when you saw them?

Respuestas: 1) What were you doing at 8 pm yesterday? · 2) Where were they going when you saw them?
📝 Ejercicio 4 — Writing / Speaking
Describe what you were doing yesterday at 3 pm, 7 pm, and 10 pm.
Example: At 3 pm, I was studying. At 7 pm, I was playing video games. At 10 pm, I was watching TV.
SUMMARY

Resumen — Fórmulas clave

11 / 12
⏳ Past Continuous
Afirmativa: S + was/were + V-ing
Negativa: S + wasn't/weren't + V-ing
Yes/No: Was/Were + S + V-ing?
WH-: WH- + was/were + S + V-ing?

I / He / She / It → was
You / We / They → were
🔗 When / While
While + past continuous → acción larga
When + past simple → acción corta

I was watching TV when the phone rang.
While I was studying, he was playing.

Expresiones: at 8 pm, last night, at that moment, yesterday at noon.
